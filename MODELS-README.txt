ZYN CITY - adding your own 3D models
1. Open index.html, press Play menu > Settings > My Models (import).
2. For each group (Cars, Buildings, People, Police car) tap "add .glb" and pick one or more .glb files.
3. If cars drive sideways/backwards, tap "Car front faces" until the front is correct.
4. Press "Apply (reload game)". Models are saved on this device (IndexedDB).
Tips: use .glb files (not .gltf with extra files), no Draco compression, low-poly (cars under ~30k triangles, buildings under ~10k).
People models with an animation clip will walk; without one they slide. Cars are scaled to 4.5 m, buildings to fit a 24 m lot, people to 1.75 m.
